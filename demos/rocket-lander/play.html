The plant.  Planar rocket booster, state [y, z, θ, ψ, ẏ, ż, θ̇, ψ̇], constant mass. You command thrust fT (0… N) and gimbal torque τ (± N·m); the gimbal angle ψ is a state you steer. Dynamics integrated with fixed-step RK4 at 50 Hz. Full state is available to your controller. Everything runs in your browser — Python via Pyodide (WebAssembly) is fetched only if you switch languages.

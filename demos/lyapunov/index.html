How it works.  A Lyapunov function V(x) is an “energy” that must stay positive and decrease along every trajectory for the equilibrium to be stable. The surface is V(x); its floor and skin are tinted green where V̇ = ∇V·f < 0 (energy draining) and red where V̇ > 0. A trajectory (integrated with RK4) is drawn both in the x-y plane and lifted onto the surface, so you can watch the state slide downhill. On the right, V(x(t)) falls monotonically exactly when the whole path stays in the green. Everything runs client-side.
